# Passkey-X package matrix

| Package | Intended customer | Members | Workspaces | Organization controls |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free | Individual evaluation | 1 | 1 | None |
| Personal | Individual | 1 | 5 | None |
| Family | Household | 6 | 20 | Household sharing |
| Team | One small workgroup | 50 | 100 | Workspace roles, missions and approvals |
| Business | Multi-department office | 500 | 500 | Departments, teams, groups, delegated admins, inherited policy and employee lifecycle |

The numeric limits are technical entitlement caps, not approved commercial prices.
Business pricing remains unconfigured until Vlightsoft approves monthly and annual
INR/USD Price amounts in Stripe test mode.

For an office that needs departments, multiple teams, employee administration and
joiner/mover/leaver workflows, choose **Business**. Choose **Team** only when one
workgroup can be governed through workspace roles without an organization hierarchy.
